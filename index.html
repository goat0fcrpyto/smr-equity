Model Stack

Nothing trades unscored.

Three proprietary models read every setup before capital moves. Their judgment trains the fourth.

Risk Filter

Custos

The gatekeeper.

A frozen baseline that scores every entry and turns away seven out of ten. Its job is not to find winners. Its job is to make sure the losers never get in.

Adaptive Model

Janus

It forgets what stopped working.

Retrained every morning on everything the market did the day before. Each version is frozen at birth and measured against the one that came before it. Patterns that fade are dropped without sentiment.

High Conviction

Praetor

Silent, until it is certain.

The same mind as Custos with a far higher bar. Praetor passes on almost everything and speaks only when the score sits at the very top. Few trades. No hesitation.

Trained on their judgment
AQUILA

Aquila

Trained by the three before it.

Every call the stack has made is a lesson for Aquila. Each setup Custos turned away, each pattern Janus let go, each entry Praetor approved. It waits until a token has proven it can survive, then commits, and lets the strongest runs travel.
